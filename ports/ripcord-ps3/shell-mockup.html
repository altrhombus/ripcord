Ripcord — proposed PS3 shell

A mockup of SHELL-DESIGN.md. Arrow keys move the cursor. The typeface here is whatever your system has; the real thing is Rodin, read off the console's flash.

What to judge. The wave's speed and contrast (it should be barely noticeable), the overshoot when the cursor lands on a card, and whether the console name reads from across a room. Enter button flips confirm between ✕ and ○ — the console tells us which it uses, and hardcoding one is a choice to be wrong for some of the people using it.
